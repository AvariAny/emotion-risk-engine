# Project Vision

## Problem

Current chatbots and conversational AI systems can generate responses without understanding whether a user is experiencing emotional distress.

In critical situations, such as anxiety, depression, loneliness, or suicidal ideation, this lack of awareness can lead to inappropriate or unsafe responses.

There is a need for an intelligent module capable of estimating emotional risk before generating a response.

---

## Solution

Emotion Risk Engine is an AI backend that analyzes user messages and estimates an emotional risk level.

Instead of replacing existing chatbots, it acts as an independent layer that evaluates conversations and provides risk information that other systems can use.

---

## Target Users

- AI Chatbots
- Mental health research projects
- Educational platforms
- Customer support systems
- Healthcare applications

---

## Core Idea

The engine receives text and returns:

- Risk level
- Confidence score
- Detected emotions
- Explanation of the prediction
- Recommended action

---

## Long-Term Vision

Develop a modular emotional intelligence engine that can be integrated into any conversational AI system.
